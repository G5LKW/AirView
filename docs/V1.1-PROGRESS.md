Not done: an alert-rule condition for RAs; RAs in the public DTO.

## 3. Mode S EHS weather

The station already derived winds and temperatures from BDS 5,0/6,0 and BDS 4,4 (vertical profiles). New:

- `met.BuildGrid` / `GET /api/met/grid`: the stored observations of the last N minutes at one flight level on a
  latitude/longitude grid: vector-mean wind (opposite winds cancel), temperature reduced to the level with the
  standard lapse rate, ISA deviation, counts. Nothing interpolated.
- BDS 4,5 hazard reports (turbulence, wind shear, microburst, icing, wake vortex, SAT, pressure, radio height):
  tracker hook -> in-memory log (one per aircraft per minute, last 2000) -> `GET /api/met/hazards`. BDS 4,5 has
  no identifying bits, so these are rare and shown as received, never used in profiles; implausible SAT rejected.
- Map layer "Winds aloft" with an FL100..FL390 selector (arrows towards where the wind blows, dots coloured by
  ISA deviation, labels when zoomed in).

Not done: fronts and forecasting; hazard persistence; a hazards list in the UI (API only).

**Tests:** grid (vector mean, lapse reduction, ISA, band / window / min-obs filters), hazard log thinning and
ordering; tracker / server / engine suites.

## 6. Learned flight prediction

New package `internal/flowlearn`. From every fresh real position (1 Hz, live picture) it learns, per 0.1-degree
cell, altitude band and 30-degree heading sector, how traffic turned over the next 30 and 60 s (time-decayed,
half-life 3 h, so it follows the runway in use and current routings). Prediction integrates those turn rates.
Self-correction: every 15 s per aircraft a learned and a straight prediction are made for 30 / 60 s and scored
when the aircraft gets there; the blend weight moves towards the closer method, and the errors are reported.
`GET /api/prediction/learned` (stats), `learned` in `GET /api/prediction/path/{icao}`, and a dotted
"Learned from traffic" line for the selected aircraft on the map.

Measured on a synthetic arrival turn: 60-s error 0.29 km learned vs 2.63 km straight; self-check improvement
66 % (30 s) / 73 % (60 s), weight -> 1. With straight traffic it stays straight.

Not done: blending into the trajectory filter's coasting positions (the live "predicted" positions still come
from the IMM filter alone); persistence across restarts; per-cell weights.

**Tests:** learning and self-correction on a synthetic turn, straight traffic stays straight, empty learner is
dead reckoning, benchmark (390 ns per observation).

## 4. MLAT output with Radarcape GPS clocks

GPS frames of class `gps_utc` are sent as nanoseconds of the UTC day moved to the start of the frame (64 µs short,
120 µs long; MLATC.md §5.1; the legacy 1..86400 firmware second is already corrected by the Beast ingest), with
`clock_type: radarcape_gps`. The handshake follows the inputs' clock: 12 MHz before the first timed frame, and a
reconnect with the matching type when only frames of the other clock arrived for a minute. GPS frames without lock
or UTC (`gps_emul`, `gps_gpst`) and frames of a second clock are counted and skipped; the Settings status card
shows the clock and both counters. **Tests:** `TestMLATOutGPSClock` (fake server: reconnect as radarcape_gps,
values, the midnight wrap, no-lock frames skipped); the server side of the same offsets is `internal/ingest/mlatc`.
**Not done:** a test against a real network (the owner has not asked to feed one); UDP transport; `zlib2`.

## 5. Learned prediction in the coasting filter

The tracker asks `flowlearn.Learner.Blend` where the learned turns take a coasting aircraft from its last real fix
and moves the IMM position towards it by the self-check weight of the horizon times the mean cell confidence; only
after >= 50 scored predictions and a measured gain of >= 10 % and >= 100 m over dead reckoning (on straight traffic
the "gain" was 0 vs 2 m and is correctly ignored). New setting `prediction.learnedTurns` (default on, Settings >
Prediction), DTO `predLearned` shown in the detail panel. On the synthetic arrival turn: 0.12 km blended vs 0.55 km
straight after 40 s. **Not done:** persistence across restarts; `/api/prediction` lists still show the filter alone.
